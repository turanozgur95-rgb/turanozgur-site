Cipher Cadets – Support

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Cipher Cadets: 11+ Practice (iPhone and iPad) is a code-breaking practice game for children, made by Tamakala Games. Need a hand? Write to us and we will reply by email.

info@tamakalagames.com

Please tell us your device (for example, iPad 10th generation), its iOS or iPadOS version and what happened. Please do not include your child's name or other personal details; we only use your email to reply and do not keep it longer than needed.

Questions and answers

  • Is any data collected? No. There are no accounts, adverts or analytics, and the app works offline. Read the Privacy Policy.
  • Where is my child's progress kept? Only on the device. If you delete the app, the progress is deleted too. Grown-ups can manage settings in the Dossier, which is behind a parental gate.
  • How does the purchase work? The app is a single purchase on the App Store, with no subscription and no in-app purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • New device? Download the app again from your App Store purchase history using the same Apple Account. Progress does not move between devices.

Please note

Cipher Cadets is an independent practice game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any exam board, test publisher, school or local authority. All questions are original, and the game cannot promise any result in a real exam.
