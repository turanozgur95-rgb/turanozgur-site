Cipher Cadets – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Cipher Cadets: 11+ Practice (iOS and iPadOS) is a code-breaking practice game for children made by Tamakala Games.

  • The app does not collect, store off the device or share any personal data.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.
  • The app has no network access and works completely offline.
  • Game progress and the grown-ups' Dossier (results by area and question type, an optional school year and exam month, and a generated code name) are stored only on the device and are deleted when the app is deleted. Nothing is ever sent to us.
  • The app does not ask for any permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, notifications, etc.).
  • If a grown-up chooses to save a certificate of promotion, the picture is saved only to the device, where the grown-up chooses.
  • The grown-ups' area is behind a parental gate. There are no links out of the app.
  • The app is designed for children and follows Apple's Kids Category guidelines.

Cipher Cadets is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any exam board, test publisher, school or local authority. All questions are original and generated by the app itself.
