Frequently asked questions

There is no sound.
Make sure the speaker button at the top right shows 🔊 and your device volume is up.

Can I move my progress to another device?
No. To protect your privacy we collect no data; progress is stored only on the device you play on.

Does the game need the internet?
No, the game works fully offline.
