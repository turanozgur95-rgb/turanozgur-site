Brainosaur – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Brainosaur: Gifted Test Prep (Android, iOS and iPadOS) is a game for children made by Tamakala Games (Turan Özgür).

  • The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.
  • The app works completely offline and does not use the internet.
  • Game progress and the parent report (skills, test date, practice-test results) are stored only on the device and are deleted when the app is deleted.
  • The app does not ask for any permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, etc.).
  • The parent area is behind a parental gate.
  • The app is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy and Apple's Kids Category guidelines.

Brainosaur is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any test publisher or school district.
