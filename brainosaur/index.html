Brainosaur: Gifted Test Prep

A dinosaur adventure for kids ages 5–9 with picture, number and shape puzzles in the style of US gifted & talented (G&T) screening tests.

  • 66 story adventures across 12 time periods
  • 14 question types in three parts: words & pictures, numbers, shapes
  • Every question is read aloud: no reading needed
  • Difficulty adapts to each child; a friendly mammoth explains how to think after a mistake
  • Test-Ready Report for parents, behind a parent gate
  • Practice tests in the Great Portal
  • One-time purchase. No ads, no subscription, no data collected. Works offline.
