Mock test

Pick a test, pick the grade, press Start. You get a results page and the answers at the end.

In short: a free online gifted practice test for K–3 in four forms: a 12-question quick check, a CogAT-style screener (picture analogies, number analogies and figure matrices), and an NNAT-style shape test of 24 or 48 questions with a timer. Every test uses new original questions and ends with a results page by question type and step-by-step answers.

Questions parents ask

How long is the mock test?

The quick check has 12 questions with no timer. The CogAT-style screener has 3 parts of 8 questions with no timer. The NNAT-style shape test has 24 questions in 15 minutes, or the full length of 48 questions in 30 minutes.

Is the result an official score?

No. It is a practice result that shows which question types to practice next. It does not predict a test score or a placement.

Is my child's result saved?

Only in your own browser on this device, so you can compare tests. Nothing is sent to us.
