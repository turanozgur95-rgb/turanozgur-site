Picture Odd One Out practice

Six pictures: which one does not belong? · NGAT Verbal (style), OLSAT-style picture classification

In short: Odd one out shows six pictures. Five share one idea and one does not. Your child finds the picture that does not belong. This is the format of the NGAT Verbal test, and the same thinking is used in OLSAT-style classification.

How to solve it in 3 steps

  1. Name all six pictures quickly.
  2. Find a group that fits five of them. Make it exact.
  3. The picture outside the group is the answer. Check that the other five really fit.

What is a picture odd one out question?

Five of the six pictures belong to one exact group (for example, things that fly), and one is close but does not fit (a penguin is a bird but does not fly). The child has to find the group first, then the picture outside it.

Common mistakes

  • Picking the picture that looks different (color, size) instead of the one that means something different.
  • Using a group that fits all six, or only four.
  • Not checking all six.

10 minutes at home

Toy box game: line up 4 toys that share something and add one that doesn't. Swap roles and let your child make one for you.

Questions parents ask

What is the NGAT Verbal test?

The Naglieri General Ability Tests (NGAT) Verbal part shows pictures and asks which one does not share the same idea. Instructions are given in short animations, so children do not need to read.

How many answer choices are there?

In this format six pictures are shown, and the child chooses the one that does not belong.

Is this the same as CogAT picture classification?

It is the reverse of it: classification finds what belongs, odd one out finds what does not. Practicing both helps.
