Paper Folding practice

Paper is folded and punched. What does it look like open? · CogAT (Nonverbal Battery)

In short: In paper folding a square paper is folded once or twice, then a hole is punched or a shape is cut. Your child picks how the paper looks when it is opened. Every fold doubles the holes and mirrors them across the fold line. It is part of the CogAT Nonverbal Battery.

How to solve it in 3 steps

  1. Follow the folds in order and see how many layers are under the hole.
  2. Unfold one fold at a time: mirror the holes across that line.
  3. Count the holes and check where they are.

What is a paper folding question?

The key ideas are: each hole goes through every layer, and when you unfold, the holes appear mirrored across the fold line. Two folds can make four holes.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting that a hole goes through all the layers.
  • Mirroring to the wrong side of the line.
  • Unfolding in the wrong order.

10 minutes at home

Do it for real: fold paper, punch with a pencil, guess, then open. Kids learn this fastest with real paper.

Questions parents ask

What age is paper folding for?

It appears on CogAT nonverbal tests from the early grades. Most children find it hard at first; real paper practice helps a lot.

How many holes can there be?

One fold gives 2 holes from 1 punch, two folds give up to 4.
