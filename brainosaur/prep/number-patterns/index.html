Number Patterns practice

Find the rule in a row or grid of numbers. · NGAT Quantitative (style), CogAT Number Series (grade 3+)

In short: Number patterns show a row or a grid of numbers that follow a rule. Your child finds the rule and the missing number. This matches the NGAT Quantitative test and the CogAT Number Series subtest from grade 3.

How to solve it in 3 steps

  1. Write the jumps between the numbers.
  2. Look for a rule that fits every jump.
  3. Use it for the missing place and check the whole row again.

What is a number patterns question?

Rules include adding or subtracting the same number, doubling, growing steps, and patterns that go across rows and down columns in a grid.

Common mistakes

  • Checking only two numbers.
  • Missing that the jumps themselves grow.
  • In grids, reading only across and not down.

10 minutes at home

Car game: one person says three numbers that follow a rule (3, 6, 9), the other says the next one and the rule.

Questions parents ask

What is the NGAT Quantitative test?

It is the number part of the Naglieri General Ability Tests. It uses numbers and symbols in patterns, not word problems.

At what age do children see number grids?

Usually grade 2 and up. Start with rows, then grids.

How many choices are there?

Our practice uses 5 choices, like the NGAT format.
