Sentence Completion (Listen & Pick) practice

Listen to a question and pick the picture that answers it. · CogAT (Verbal Battery, K–2)

In short: In sentence completion the child listens to a short question, such as “Which one do we use to cut paper?”, and picks the picture that answers it. It tests listening and everyday word knowledge. It is part of the CogAT Verbal Battery at kindergarten to grade 2. Tap Read to me to hear each question.

How to solve it in 3 steps

  1. Listen to the whole question. Notice small words like not, most or before.
  2. Say the question again in your own words.
  3. Look at every picture and pick the one that answers the question best.

What is a sentence completion question?

The question is read aloud once. The child keeps it in mind and looks at the pictures. Questions use everyday ideas: what things are used for, where things are, what happens first or last, and words like not, most and before.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing the first picture that is linked to a word in the question.
  • Missing the word “not”.
  • Not waiting until the question is finished.

10 minutes at home

Play “I'm thinking of…” at dinner: “I'm thinking of something we use to eat soup.” Add tricky words: “…something that is not hot.”

Questions parents ask

Is the CogAT sentence completion test read aloud?

Yes. At the picture levels the teacher or the tablet reads each question, and the child answers by choosing a picture. Here the browser reads the question when you tap Read to me.

Why does my child get easy questions wrong?

Usually they answer before the end of the question or miss a word like “not”. Ask them to repeat the question before answering.

Which voice reads the questions?

Your device's built-in voice. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
