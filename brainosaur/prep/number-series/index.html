Number Series (Bead Patterns) practice

Beads on rods follow a pattern. What comes next? · CogAT (Quantitative Battery)

In short: Number series shows rods with beads, like an abacus. The number of beads follows a pattern (2, 4, 6…). Your child picks the rod that comes next. It is the picture form of the CogAT Number Series subtest for young children.

How to solve it in 3 steps

  1. Count the beads on each rod and say the numbers: 2, 4, 6.
  2. Find the jump between them: +2 each time.
  3. Use the jump to find the next number and pick the rod with that many beads.

What is a number series question?

Patterns can go up, go down, repeat, or change by a growing step. At higher levels two patterns can be mixed (one rod up, the next one down).

Common mistakes

  • Looking at the colors of the beads instead of the count.
  • Using the first jump only when the jumps change.
  • Counting a rod twice.

10 minutes at home

Build bead or block towers that grow by 2. Ask your child to build the next one, then make a pattern for you.

Questions parents ask

What patterns are in number series questions?

Adding or taking away the same number, repeating patterns, and at higher levels jumps that grow (+1, +2, +3) or two patterns taking turns.

Does my child need to know numbers above 20?

No. Bead patterns for young children stay small; counting carefully matters more.

What if two answers seem right?

Check the pattern on every rod, not just the last two. Only one answer fits all of them.
