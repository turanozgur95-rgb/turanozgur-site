Questions parents ask

What is Brainosaur Prep?

Brainosaur Prep is a free practice hub by Tamakala Games for parents of children in kindergarten to grade 3 who are preparing for gifted and talented (G&T) screening tests such as the CogAT, NNAT3, NGAT and OLSAT. It has unlimited practice questions in 14 question types, printable PDF worksheets with answer keys, a guide to each test and a mock test.

Is it really free?

Yes. Practice questions, worksheets and the mock test are free and need no account. The Brainosaur app is a separate one-time purchase.

Are these real CogAT or NNAT questions?

No. Every question is original and made by our own question generator in the style of the test. Real test questions are not public. Tamakala Games is not affiliated with any test publisher.

What age is this for?

Children aged 5 to 9 (kindergarten to grade 3). Choose K–1, Grade 2 or Grade 3 above each set of questions.

Can practice guarantee a result?

No. Practice helps a child get used to the question formats and the way of thinking. No practice can guarantee a test result or a gifted placement.

Is my child's data collected?

No. Scores are stored only in your own browser. There are no cookies, ads or tracking.
