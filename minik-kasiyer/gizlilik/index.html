Little Cashier – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Little Cashier (Android, iOS, iPadOS and Google Play Games) is a game for children made by Tamakala Games (Turan Özgür).

  • The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.
  • The app works completely offline; it does not use the internet.
  • Game progress (stars, chosen character, settings) is saved only on the device and is deleted when the app is removed.
  • The app does not ask for any permissions (camera, microphone, location, contacts, etc.).
  • The app is designed for children and complies with Google Play's Families Policy and Apple's Kids Category guidelines.

Contact: info@tamakalagames.com
